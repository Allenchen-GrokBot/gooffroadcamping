下班越野露營趣（GoOffRoad Camping）是台灣的 ADV 重機越野露營社群。不論你騎哪個品牌的 ADV 多功能車，都歡迎加入我們，一起探索台灣密林美景，跑林道、過溪、玩沙，晚上一起重機露營、野營。我們也有騎乘練習活動、Taiwan ADV Trophy 交流賽和出國騎車旅遊，並在 YouTube 分享每次的騎乘與活動紀錄。下班後，一起輕鬆越野、快樂露營！

GoOffRoad Camping (下班越野露營趣) is a Taiwan ADV motorcycle off-road and camping community. Whatever adventure bike you ride, come join us to explore Taiwan's lush forests and hidden scenery, ride forest roads, cross rivers and play in the sand, then camp together under the stars. We also run riding practice sessions, host the Taiwan ADV Trophy, organize overseas motorcycle tours, and share our rides and events on YouTube. Come ride off-road and camp happily with us!
